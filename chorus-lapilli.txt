Started transition from tic tac toe to chorus lapilli by implementing phase detection. Added 
logic to count a player's pieces on the board. if they reach 3, the game enters movement phase 
where they can click to select their piece. Updated the Square component to accept an isSelected
prop to highlight the chosen piece in yellow.

Added the two click movement mechanism for the movement phase. selecting an owned piece saves 
its index and clicking an empty square moves the piece by updating the board array and advancing
the turn. Clicking an occupied square automatically drops the selection to enforce the invalid 
move requirement.
